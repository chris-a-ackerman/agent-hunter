Card variants (`experiments/hw7/cards/`, generated by `experiments/hw7/card_variants.py`, see its README): `original` = the frozen cards; `inflated` = every card has Quillbase's description, own price; `price_shuffled` = original descriptions, prices permuted with seed 0.

Notes: SQ = real accuracy of the hire ÷ the best provider's, per job, then averaged over jobs (profile-reading: the mean of its runs per job; a failed run scores 0). Cheapest and most expensive are recomputed from each variant's card prices. Trial-then-Hire (6 invoices, AGE-39 change on) never reads card text and breaks ties on measured trial cost, so its rows are the same for every variant. Invoices correct = expected held-out invoices scored ≥ 0.90, summed over jobs.
